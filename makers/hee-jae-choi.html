Hee Jae Choi

Cremona, Italy
Violins; repair

Hee Jae Choi is studying violin making at the Istituto Stradivari in Cremona. In 2024 she won First Prize at the Violin Making Competition for Young Luthiers in Pisogne. Alongside her studies she has trained in several workshops, including LA Violin Shop in Los Angeles, building experience in both making and repair within the Cremonese tradition.
