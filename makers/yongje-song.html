Yongje Song

Seoul, Korea
Violin, viola and cello bows

A graduate of the Chicago School of Violin Making, Yongje Song makes bows in the French tradition from his workshop in Seoul. At the 2018 VSA competition he received a Double Gold Medal for his violin and cello bows and a Certificate of Merit for his viola bow. Before setting up independently he worked at J.B. Lee’s shop in Seoul, where he also taught bow making.
