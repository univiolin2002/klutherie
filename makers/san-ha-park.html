San-ha Park

Corona, California, United States
Acoustic and electric guitars; repair and restoration of all stringed instruments

San-ha Park studied Guitar Craft and Acoustic Guitar Design at the Musicians Institute in Hollywood and apprenticed with the guitar maker Kathy Wingert, who encouraged him to take up violin making. For several years he built violins and violas alongside guitars, winning several awards at the Violin Makers Association of Arizona International competition, including the 2021 Gold Medal for Violin Tone. He has since returned to guitar making as his main work, building commissioned acoustic and electric guitars from his workshop in Corona, California, where he also repairs and restores stringed instruments of all kinds.
