Jake Jiwon Han

Los Angeles, United States
Violins and violas; acoustic adjustment and restoration

Jake Jiwon Han has been Head Luthier at LA Violin Shop since 2015, where he specialises in acoustic adjustment and the restoration of fine instruments. At the Violin Society of America competitions he received a Silver Medal for Tone (violin) in 2022 and Certificates of Merit for Tone for viola in 2022 and violin in 2018. His instruments are played by professional and advanced players.
